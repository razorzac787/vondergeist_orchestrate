# VONDERGEIST 

Let us start with a few incidents to begin with... 

##Onavo 
Facebook ended its unpaid market research programs and proactively took its Onavo VPN app off the Google Play Store in the wake of backlash following TechCrunch’s investigation about Onavo code being used in a Facebook Research app that sucked up data about teens.

##Ashley Madison
In July 2015, an unknown person or group calling itself "The Impact Team" announced that they had stolen user data of Ashley Madison, a commercial website billed as enabling extramarital affairs. The hackers copied personal information about the site's user base and threatened to release names and personal identifying information if Ashley Madison would not immediately shut down. To underscore the validity of the threat, personal information of more than 2,500 users was released. Ashley Madison denied that its records were insecure and continued to operate.

##ShinyHunters Data Breaches... one of them and the most recent ones 
Kodak confirmed a June 2026 data breach after ShinyHunters claimed it stole 2.2 million customer and corporate records. ShinyHunters listed Kodak on its dark web leak site on June 15 and set a June 18 deadline before threatening to leak the alleged data.

Data breaches are something that companies are scared of because a small bug in your codebase could result in violating the privacy of millions of people who trust these companies to 
protect its data and affect people's lives. While some companies are now much aware about protecting their client's data and are implementing their own privacy measures, many of the companies still face these issues and are surprisingly more frequent 

That is why we created Vondergeist - Multi-agent system that scans a codebase/repo/cloud config for breach risks, scores them, and lets you chat with an AI to understand and fix each one -like Copilot, but for security posture.
